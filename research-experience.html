B.Eng. Thesis Topic: Multiphysics-effected iontronic behaviors in flexible batteries

Flexible batteries facilitate epidermal self-powered healthcare but suffer from dendrite growth and thermal runaway. Herein, I developed an ultrathin zinc lattice electrode with biomimetic microhole arrays, enabling spatially selective deposition at engineered iontronic interfaces. I further proposed a zone-based design optimization approach for battery thermal management to suppress performance degradation. My multiphysics regulation strategy delivers 2,000-hour cycle stability in flexible cells, paving the way for wearable healthcare.
